Concept · CRM / SaaS

Helping teams manage customers and sales in one place.

Businesses often struggle to manage customer data, communicate efficiently, and track client interactions. Curve is a self-directed concept exploring a centralized, intuitive CRM for small teams who find tools like Salesforce or HubSpot too heavy for their size.

01

The Problem

Disorganized customer data across multiple systems and inefficient communication channels lead to limited insight into client interactions.

That combination often results in missed sales opportunities and reduced customer satisfaction — problems most CRM tools try to solve by adding more features rather than removing friction.

  • Centralize client data for easy access
  • Automate repetitive workflows and tasks
  • Surface insight through analytics without overwhelming the user
  • Manage orders, products, customers, and finances in one consistent system

My Role

As the designer on this concept, I focused on designing intuitive workflows and scalable interfaces — developing high-fidelity onboarding and dashboard screens, and designing key workflows for managing orders, products, customers, and analytics.

The throughline across every screen was interface consistency and usability across modules, with responsive layouts throughout.

04

Intended Impact

  • Aims to help small teams build stronger client relationships without CRM overwhelm
  • Designed to increase sales efficiency and reduce manual errors
  • Supports data-driven decisions without requiring a dedicated ops person to maintain it
  • A concept exploration, not a validated outcome — see reflection below

Reflection

Curve was a study in subtraction — most CRM tools compete by adding features, and the more interesting design problem was figuring out what a small team could safely live without.

If I were building this for real users, I'd want to pressure-test that minimalism against actual sales workflows — subtraction is easy to defend in a concept project and much harder to get right when a real sales team's edge cases show up.
